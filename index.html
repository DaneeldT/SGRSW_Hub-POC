Tab 05 · SGRSW As-Built Protocol

As-built Requirements

Protocols relating to as-built and close-out requirements by Roads Infrastructure Management, Urban Mobility Directorate.

The following provides an overview of the standard as-built and close-out requirements. Section 19.2 of the Standards and Guidelines for Roads & Stormwater shall be consulted for comprehensive requirements.

As-built and close-out information shall be submitted to the relevant RIM District on completion of the contract. The information and documents shall be provided via email or an accessible online platform. The following will be required:

Communication/Correspondence relating to any design or material changes (supported by RIM) during construction.

Submitted for all inspections conducted, i.e. intermediate, completion and final (after the 12-month DLP).
  • As-Built drawings/data shall be provided via email or an accessible online platform. Hard copies shall be submitted only upon request;
  • Provide separate drawings for each service: roads (incl. sections & pavement design), stormwater, road signs and markings, combined services;
  • Clearly mark drawing status as “AS-BUILT”; use revision “Z1” for the first submission and “Z2”, “Z3”, etc. for subsequent submissions;
  • Indicate service ownership (i.e. “Municipal” or “Private”), incl. the extent;
  • Drawing formats: PDF, DWG (WGS84 co-ordinate system; no XREFs), TIFF;
  • Drawings drawn BY LAYER (and not BY COLOR). Use descriptive layer names;
  • Include a legend & table containing a co-ordinate list (Label, X, Y) of all stormwater infrastructure (manholes, catchpits, headwalls, sub-surface drain rodding eyes, etc.), with invert & cover levels. Submit as Excel file as well;
  • New infrastructure to be indicated as “new” (not “proposed”);
  • Spatial: ESRI File Geodatabase or Shape files (*.shp);
  • Attributes to include pipe diameters, length, capacity, material, feature id, feature name, etc.;
  • Survey and LandXML (if requested).
  • Only populate infrastructure (assets) the City will take ownership of;
  • Submit in MS Excel format.
  • Include a Material Test Result Summary report of the conducted tests and corresponding results. Refer to example template;
  • As-built materials records shall be submitted in accordance with TMH10. The submission shall include the prescribed TMH10 as-built records, together with relevant design documentation (including standard cross-sections, pavement designs and approved mix designs), a register of conditionally accepted and rejected work, and any other information required by the Approval Authority. The Approval Authority shall confirm the required as-built sheets and submission requirements prior to report production.

Templates: TMH10 · Material Test Result Summary
  • Video footage and PDF reports;
  • Confirmation of Review Letter from the Consulting Engineer.

Shall include as-built drawings of all structures within the road reserve, incl. inventory and condition inspection data in accordance with TMH19 (completed by a COTO-registered Bridge Inspector), any required fully signed bridge agreements & supporting documentation (Asset Register, Asset Handover Cert).

Templates: Annexure A · RSW-D3-HC-02 · TMH19

Provide a photo report of new infrastructure (if required).

Stormwater deliverables as required by CSRM (if applicable).

Practical Completion, Completion and Final Approval certificates (signed).
  • Only required for new municipal infrastructure (not private infrastructure);
  • Where new municipal infrastructure includes structures (e.g. retaining walls, bridges), a separate Asset Handover Certificate shall be completed therefor.
